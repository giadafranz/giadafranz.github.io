### Literature and useful resources

- [CM11] T. Colding, W. Minicozzi, *[A course in minimal surfaces](https://bookstore.ams.org/gsm-121/)*. Graduate Studies in Mathematics, 121. American Mathematical Society, Providence, RI, 2011. xii+313 pp.
- [Whi16] B. White, *[Introduction to minimal surface theory](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.3325.pdf)*. Geometric analysis, 387-438, IAS/Park City Math. Ser., 22. American Mathematical Society, Providence, RI, 2016.
- [Cho25] O. Chodosh, *[Introduction to minimal surfaces](https://web.stanford.edu/~ochodosh/Math286-min-surf.pdf)*. Notes from the course Math 286 given at Stanford.
- [Web26] M. Weber, *[Minimal surfaces repository](https://minimalsurfaces.blog/home/repository/)*. Online collection of minimal surfaces illustrations.
